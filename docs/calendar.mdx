---
title: "Connect calendars and email"
description: "Sync events from Google Calendar, Outlook, and Apple Calendar, and configure Gmail follow-up drafts."
---

Notare connects directly to calendar and email services from your local computer via local loopback (`127.0.0.1`). There are no intermediate cloud servers or proxies; calendar and email data never pass through third-party infrastructure.

All initial calendar permissions are **read-only**.

---

## Google Calendar

Notare supports one-click connection in official builds and allows connecting multiple accounts simultaneously.

### One-click sign-in (official builds)

1. Open **Calendar** in the sidebar or go to **Settings → Calendar**.
2. Under **Google**, click **Sign in with Google**.
3. Your default browser opens Google's OAuth consent screen for Notare.
4. Select your account and grant read-only permissions (`calendar.readonly` and `calendar.events.readonly`).
5. Return to Notare. Your calendars appear within a few seconds. **Tick the calendars you want** to display.

### Multi-account support

You can connect both personal and work Google accounts:
- Click **+ Add Google account** in **Settings → Calendar** to authenticate additional accounts.
- Refresh tokens are isolated per account in your operating system's native keychain (`google-calendar/oauth/<email>`).
- Manage or disconnect individual accounts at any time by clicking **Remove** next to that account.

### "Google hasn't verified this app" screen

Until Google completes the OAuth verification review for our first-party client, you may see an **"unverified app"** notice in your browser during sign-in. This is normal while the app is in verification or testing mode:

1. Click **Advanced** in small text near the bottom left of the Google page.
2. Click **Go to Notare (unsafe)**.
3. Review the permissions and click **Continue**.

<Note>
  **7-day token expiration:** While Google keeps the OAuth client in testing mode, Google automatically expires refresh tokens after **7 days**. If Notare reports that calendar events are no longer updating, go to **Settings → Calendar** and click **Sign in with Google** again to refresh the session.
</Note>

### Bring your own Google client

If you build from source or prefer using your own Google Cloud project credentials, click **Advanced: use your own OAuth client** in **Settings → Calendar**. For step-by-step instructions on creating a Google Cloud Desktop app credential, see the [Google Calendar setup guide](GOOGLE-CALENDAR.md).

## Outlook Calendar

Notare connects directly to Microsoft 365 and personal Outlook accounts using native PKCE authentication.

### One-click sign-in (official builds)

1. Go to **Settings → Calendar** (or open the Calendar sidebar).
2. Under **Outlook**, click **Sign in with Microsoft**.
3. In your browser, log in with your Microsoft account (personal `@outlook.com` / `@hotmail.com` or corporate Microsoft 365 / Entra ID).
4. Approve the requested read-only permissions (`User.Read` and `Calendars.Read`).
5. Return to Notare and toggle the calendars you want to sync.

### Multi-account support

- Connect multiple Microsoft accounts by clicking **+ Add Microsoft account**.
- Credentials and refresh tokens are isolated per account in your OS keychain under `outlook-calendar/oauth/<email>`.
- Disconnect accounts individually using the **Remove** button.

### Bring your own Entra client ID

If your organization's IT policy restricts third-party app consents or you run a custom build, click **Advanced: use your own Application (client) ID** and enter your Entra ID client ID. See the [Outlook Calendar setup guide](OUTLOOK-CALENDAR.md) for full configuration steps.

## Apple Calendar

Apple Calendar integration is available on macOS and accesses calendar accounts configured in macOS System Settings:

1. In Notare, select **Apple Calendar** under **Settings → Calendar**.
2. Allow calendar access when macOS displays the system permission dialog.
3. Enable the calendars you want to see in Notare.

If access was previously denied, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars** on your Mac to grant permission.

## Gmail drafts (opt-in beta)

Notare includes an optional Gmail integration specifically for creating **follow-up email drafts** after meetings.

<Note>
  **Strict privacy boundaries:**
  - **Separate integration:** Connecting or disconnecting Gmail has no effect on Google Calendar.
  - **Draft-only permission (`gmail.compose`):** Notare can only create draft messages in your account. It **cannot read** your inbox, cannot read received messages, and **never automatically sends** emails.
  - **Review before sending:** Drafts appear in your Gmail drafts folder so you can review and edit them yourself before hitting send.
</Note>

### Connecting Gmail

1. Open **Settings → Integrations → Gmail (beta)**.
2. Click **Connect Account**.
3. Authorize the `gmail.compose` scope in your browser.
4. Once connected, meeting follow-up drafts generated via **••• → Follow-up email** will be created directly in your Gmail drafts.

For more technical details and security considerations, see the [Gmail integration guide](GMAIL.md).
