Notare includes an integrated AI assistant that can query your notes and transcripts. You can ask questions about the current meeting or query your entire meeting history.

Open Chat from the right sidebar or using the chat icon in any note.

## Scope selector

The scope selector in the chat toolbar controls which meetings and transcripts the AI can search and reference:

- **This meeting:** Default when viewing an open note. Restricts retrieval strictly to the current meeting note and its audio transcript.
- **Selected:** Scopes queries to a specific set of manually selected meetings.
- **Person:** Filters context to meetings involving a specific participant or attendee.
- **Organization:** Filters context to meetings involving attendees from a specific company or domain.
- **Everything:** Default when opening chat without a note open. Searches across your entire local database of meetings, notes, and transcripts.

To switch scope, click the scope button in the chat header and choose an option.

---

## Interactive citation chips

When the assistant answers questions based on meeting transcripts, it grounds facts with timestamped citations:

- Citations appear in the message as interactive chips (for example, `Meeting · 14:22`).
- Clicking a citation chip immediately opens the referenced meeting note and seeks the audio recording to that exact millisecond.
- You can listen to what was said firsthand to verify context and tone.

## Recipes

Recipes are reusable prompt templates that run common queries against your notes without retyping long instructions.

### Using recipes in chat

1. In the chat message input, type `/` at the beginning of a line or after a space.
2. The **Recipes** slash menu opens above the input box.
3. Start typing to filter recipes by title or prompt content (for example, `/action` or `/decision`).
4. Use the **Up** and **Down** arrow keys to highlight a recipe, then press **Enter** or **Tab** to apply it.
5. The recipe chip appears in your message input. Add any additional instructions if needed, then send.

### Built-in recipes

Notare includes built-in recipes ready to use:

- **Action items:** Extracts tasks, owners, and due dates from the meeting.
- **Key decisions:** Summarizes decisions made, rationale, and alternatives discussed.
- **Follow-up email draft:** Prepares a professional recap and next steps for attendees.
- **Customer objections:** Surfaces concerns raised by external participants.
- **Weekly digest:** Aggregates progress and blockers across meetings.
- **Questions I didn't ask:** Highlights unaddressed topics or gaps in the discussion.
- **Pre-meeting brief:** Synthesizes attendee context and past discussion topics.

### Managing recipes

You can create and customize recipes under **Settings → Recipes**:

1. Open **Settings → Recipes**.
2. Click **New Recipe** (or click the duplicate icon on an existing recipe to start from a template).
3. Fill in the recipe fields:
   - **Title:** The name displayed in the slash menu.
   - **Scope:** Choose **Single meeting** (runs against one note) or **Multiple meetings** (runs across multi-meeting contexts).
   - **Prompt:** The system instructions. You can include `{{selection}}` to inject highlighted text or note context.
4. Click **Save**.

Your custom recipes immediately become available in the chat `/` menu.
