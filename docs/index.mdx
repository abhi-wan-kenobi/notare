## The short version

1. Open a calendar event or create a note.
2. Click **Join & record** or **Record**.
3. Take rough notes in **Memos** while Notare listens.
4. Stop listening, then review **Summary** and **Transcript**.

Your app data is stored locally. If you choose a hosted transcription or language-model provider, the content needed for that feature is sent directly to that provider.

<Note>
  New to Notare? Open the **Welcome to Notare** note and click **Join & record**. It uses a private, prerecorded demo meeting.
</Note>
