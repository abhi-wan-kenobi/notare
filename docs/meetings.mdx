title: "Record meetings"
description: "Prepare with briefs, record without bots, and capture audio and notes locally."
---

Notare records your microphone and system audio directly from your machine without adding a bot to the call. Each meeting is stored locally with your notes, transcript, and generated summary.

## Pre-meeting briefs

Before upcoming meetings, Notare can automatically synthesize background context so you walk into the call prepared:

- **Attendee background:** Detects attendees and their organizations from calendar event invites.
- **Previous outcomes:** Summarizes the outcomes of recent discussions with the same participants, with citation chips linking to prior recordings.
- **Open action items:** Lists pending tasks and commitments from earlier meetings.
- **Suggested questions:** Generates relevant discussion topics based on past conversations.

### Configuring briefs

1. Open **Settings → Intelligence**.
2. Scroll to **Pre-Meeting Briefs** and enable **Generate pre-meeting briefs**.
3. Set your preferred **Preparation window** (how far in advance briefs are generated: 2, 4, 8, 12, or 24 hours before event start).

Briefs appear as a collapsible card on upcoming calendar event popovers and in the meeting note header. You can click the refresh icon to regenerate a brief or click **X** to dismiss it.

---

## Start recording

Choose the path that matches the meeting:

- **Calendar event with a meeting link:** Open the event note and click **Join & record**. Notare opens the call in your browser or conferencing app and starts listening.
- **In-person meeting or call already open:** Open the event note and click **Record**.
- **Unscheduled conversation:** Create a **New Note** and click **Record**.

Notare captures system audio (the other speakers) and your microphone locally.

<Warning>
  Make sure you have permission to record. Consent requirements vary depending on your local jurisdiction and participant locations.
</Warning>

---

## During the meeting

- **Memos:** Type rough notes while the call is in progress. The generated summary combines your typed notes with the full conversation transcript.
- **Transcript:** Follow the live transcript if your transcription model supports real-time decoding. Models marked **After recording** process audio after you click stop.
- **Floating bar:** Enable **Show floating bar** in **Settings → App** to monitor audio levels and control recording from a minimal overlay window.

---

## Finish the meeting

Click **Stop listening** when the call ends:

1. Audio is saved according to your retention settings (**Settings → Storage**).
2. The final transcript is reconciled and saved.
3. Your configured Intelligence model generates a structured summary.

If a meeting restarts or extends after stopping, click **Resume** from the top-right controls to continue in the same note.

---

## Use an existing recording

To transcribe or summarize pre-recorded audio:

1. Create or open an empty note.
2. Click the **•••** overflow menu in the note header.
3. Choose **Upload audio** (supports common audio files) or **Upload transcript**.
