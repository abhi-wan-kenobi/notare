Every meeting in Notare includes three integrated views:

- **Memos:** Your typed notes and scratchpad.
- **Summary:** Structured notes and action items generated by your configured Intelligence model.
- **Transcript:** The full conversation text with speaker labels and search.

You can edit memos and summaries directly. In the transcript, click any speaker label to assign participant names, or select **Apply to all** to update all matching speaker segments.

---

## Template gallery

Notare includes a built-in gallery of 25 templates designed for specific meeting types. Templates structure your AI summaries with relevant headings, checklists, and prompts.

### Browsing and applying templates

- **From an open note:** Click the template selector beside the **Summary** tab to change the structure of the current note.
- **From the sidebar:** Open the **Templates** tab to browse the complete library by category, preview sections, or create new custom templates.

### Included template categories

The gallery provides ready-to-use templates across several domains:

- **1:1 and Management:** 1:1, Performance review, Therapy / coaching session.
- **Sales and Customer Success:** Sales discovery, Sales demo, Customer success check-in, Vendor evaluation.
- **Engineering and Product:** Standup, Sprint planning, Sprint retro, Design review, Architecture review, Incident postmortem, Project kickoff, Project status.
- **Executive and Strategy:** All-hands, Board meeting, Investor update, OKR review, Legal / contract review.
- **Interviews and Research:** Interview (hiring), User research interview, Lecture / class, Podcast / interview recording, Personal journal.

### Creating custom templates

You can create custom templates tailored to your team's workflows:

1. Open **Templates** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New Template**.
3. Add a title, category, description, and custom sections with prompt instructions.
4. Save your template to make it available in the template picker.

## Follow-up email drafts

After a meeting, Notare can automatically draft a follow-up email to external attendees:

1. Open the meeting note.
2. Click the **•••** overflow menu in the note header and choose **Follow-up email**.
3. Notare extracts external participants:
   - Excludes your own email address.
   - Excludes colleagues sharing your company's internal email domain.
   - Caps recipients at 10 external contacts.
4. Your configured notes model generates a concise draft containing discussion highlights, key decisions, and assigned action items.

### Delivery methods

- **Gmail drafts (recommended):** If you connected Gmail under **Settings → Integrations → Gmail (beta)**, Notare automatically creates an editable draft in your Gmail account (`users.drafts.create`). You can review and edit it in Gmail before sending.
- **Mail client fallback:** If Gmail is not connected, Notare copies the generated email text to your system clipboard and opens your operating system's default email client with a pre-filled `mailto:` draft.

## Exporting notes

To take notes out of Notare:

1. Open the **•••** menu in the meeting header.
2. Choose **Export**.
3. Select your desired format: **PDF**, **Plain Text**, **Markdown**, or **Org-mode**.
4. Choose which sections to include: Memo, Summary, or Transcript.

Exported files are saved to your Downloads folder and revealed in your file manager.
