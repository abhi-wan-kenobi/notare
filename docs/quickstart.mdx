---
title: "Your first meeting"
description: "Set up Notare and try a private demo before your next call."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Notare">
    [Download Notare](https://github.com/abhi-wan-kenobi/notare/releases/latest), open it, and follow the setup screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow audio access">
    Allow **Microphone** so Notare can hear you. Allow **System Audio** so it can hear everyone else on the call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish the optional setup">
    Configure connected calendars, choose where Notare stores local data, or set up your language model in **Settings → Intelligence**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the private demo">
    Open **Welcome to Notare** and click **Join & record**. Notare opens a prerecorded meeting and treats it like a real call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the result">
    When the recording ends, stop listening. Open **Memos**, **Summary**, and **Transcript** to see what Notare captured.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Record a real meeting

Open a calendar event or create a note, then click **Join & record** or **Record** in the top-right corner. See [Record meetings](/meetings) for the full flow.

If Notare cannot hear the call, start with [audio troubleshooting](/troubleshooting#audio-is-missing-or-flatlined).
