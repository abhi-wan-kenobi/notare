Notare's device sync connects your computers directly using local peer-to-peer networking. Your notes and meeting data sync directly from machine to machine without passing through a cloud server or central database.

---

## How pairing works

Device pairing uses mutual cryptographic authentication with Short Authentication String (SAS) confirmation to protect against network eavesdropping and tampering.

### Pairing two devices

1. **On your first device (Offerer):**
   - Open **Settings → Devices**.
   - Click **Pair a device**.
   - A dialog opens displaying a **QR code**, a countdown timer (5-minute expiration), and a **Copy pairing link** button.

2. **On your second device (Joiner):**
   - Open **Settings → Devices**.
   - Click **Join with a link**.
   - Paste the pairing link copied from the first device and click **Join**.

3. **Verify the 6-digit code (SAS):**
   - Both devices immediately display a prominent **6-digit confirmation code** (for example, `482 195`).
   - Visually compare the code on both screens to confirm they match.
   - Click **Confirm** on both devices to establish the cryptographic trust relationship.

Once confirmed, the devices begin syncing your notes and meeting data automatically whenever they are online on the same local network.

## Device management and revocation

Under **Settings → Devices → Paired devices**, you can view all connected machines with their device labels, z-base-32 cryptographic fingerprints, and last-seen timestamps.

### Remove = Cryptographic revoke

When you click **Remove** next to a paired device:

- Notare performs a **cryptographic revocation** (`syncRevokePeer`), not merely a cosmetic removal.
- The device's public key is removed from your authorized peer store, and its pairing token is permanently invalidated.
- Even if the removed device retains a stale link or cached network offer, it cannot re-authenticate or reconnect to your machine.
