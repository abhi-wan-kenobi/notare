## App startup and crashing

### Notare doesn't start or crashes immediately (0.6.2 and 0.6.3)

If the app fails to start or shows a blank window on launch:

- **Version 0.6.2:** A plugin registration ordering issue caused an application panic at startup (`state() called before manage()`).
- **Version 0.6.3:** A background scheduler hook mounted outside the main application provider tree caused an unrecoverable render crash on launch.

**Resolution:** Update to the latest release (version 0.6.4 or later). Download the newest build from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/abhi-wan-kenobi/notare/releases/latest) or let the in-app updater apply the update.

## AI and subscription errors

### "CORS requests are not allowed for this Organization" (Claude subscription)

When using **Anthropic (Subscription)**, calls were rejected with HTTP 401 `CORS requests are not allowed for this Organization`:

- **Cause:** Versions prior to 0.6.5 sent webview origin headers (`Origin: tauri://localhost`) and browser flags that Anthropic's OAuth endpoints reject.
- **Resolution:** Fixed in **version 0.6.5**. Update to 0.6.5 or later. Notare now automatically strips origin and browser headers from subscription requests.

### ChatGPT "model not supported"

When using **OpenAI Codex (Subscription)**, requests returned a "model not supported" error:

- **Cause:** The ChatGPT Codex subscription backend requires specific model identifiers (`gpt-6.1-sol`, `gpt-6.1-terra`, `gpt-5.6-luna`) and forced SSE streaming with the Responses API. Requesting generic or standard OpenAI API model names (such as `gpt-5` or `gpt-4o`) fails.
- **Resolution:** Fixed in **version 0.6.6**. Update to 0.6.6 or later. Notare syncs supported subscription models dynamically from the catalog and manages the required Responses API streaming headers.

## Calendar and email integration

### "Google hasn't verified this app" notice

When connecting Google Calendar or Gmail in official builds:

- **Cause:** The first-party OAuth client is operating in testing mode pending Google's verification process.
- **Resolution:** Click **Advanced** at the bottom-left of the consent screen, then click **Go to Notare (unsafe)** to grant calendar permissions safely.

### Google Calendar stops refreshing after 7 days

- **Cause:** While in Google testing mode, refresh tokens expire automatically after **7 days**.
- **Resolution:** Open **Settings → Calendar** and click **Sign in with Google** to refresh your authorization. Once Google verification is finalized, refresh tokens will remain active indefinitely.

### Calendar events are missing

1. Open **Calendar** in the sidebar.
2. Confirm the account is connected and your desired calendars are checked.
3. Click the refresh icon at the top of the calendar sidebar.
4. If an account shows **Reconnect required**, disconnect and re-authenticate that account.

## Audio and transcription

### Audio is missing or flatlined

Open **Settings → Permissions** and verify:

- **Microphone:** Allowed so Notare can record your voice.
- **System Audio:** Allowed so Notare can capture other call participants.

On macOS, verify permissions in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording** and **Microphone**. Restart Notare after granting permissions.

### Transcript is not updating live

Open **Settings → Transcription** and check your active model:

- Models labeled **Live** transcribe speech in real time during the call.
- Models labeled **After recording** process the captured audio after you click **Stop listening**.

If local transcription fails, check that your graphics drivers are up to date (for GPU Vulkan transcription) or switch to a CPU-compatible Whisper model in **Settings → Transcription**.
