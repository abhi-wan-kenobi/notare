0.6.1 was tested as a release candidate only; its features shipped to everyone in 0.6.4.

## Chat

- Choose the scope of a question: This meeting, Selected meetings, a Person, an Organization or Everything
- Answers cite their meetings; click a citation to jump to that moment in the transcript
- Recipes are saved prompts: type `/` in the chat box and manage them in Settings → Recipes, with six built-in ones to start

## Providers

- Use an `omp` auth gateway as an LLM provider

## Calendar

- Sign in with Google in one click, without creating your own Google Cloud client
