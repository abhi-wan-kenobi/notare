0.6.4 replaces 0.6.2 and 0.6.3, which did not start. Everything below is local-first: nothing goes through a Notare server.

## Meeting intelligence

- On-device chat, summaries and action items with Qwen3 4B, Llama 3.1 8B, Phi-4 Mini, Mistral 7B or Gemma 3 4B; the first use downloads 2.5–5 GB
- Ask about This meeting, Selected meetings, a Person, an Organization or Everything; answers cite their meetings
- Recipes are saved prompts: type `/` in the chat box and manage them in Settings → Recipes
- Pre-meeting briefs appear on calendar events with attendees, built from your past meetings with those people
- 25 built-in note templates, from 1:1s and sales discovery to retros and board meetings
- Follow-up email drafts a recap with decisions and action items; it is copied or opened in your mail app, or saved as a Gmail draft, and is never sent automatically

## Calendars and integrations

- Sign in with Google in one click; tokens stay in your system keychain
- Connect multiple Google accounts
- Outlook and Microsoft 365 calendars connect directly
- Settings → MCP Server shares your meetings read-only with Claude Desktop, Cursor and other MCP clients
- Use an `omp` auth gateway as an LLM provider

## Also

- A trash view to restore or permanently delete notes
- A redesigned recording bar and dictation improvements
