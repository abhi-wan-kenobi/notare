## Notes

- Notes, titles and briefs made with the built-in local model no longer include the model's reasoning
- Schedule follow-up from a note's menu creates a Google or Outlook event with the attendees and action items

## Calendar

- Zoom the calendar between Month, Week, 3 days and Day with pinch, Ctrl/Cmd + scroll, or the − and + buttons; your choice is remembered
- A new calendar sign-in shows events right away, because the account's primary calendar is switched on automatically

## Integrations

- The MCP server can search your meetings by meaning for Claude Desktop and Cursor
