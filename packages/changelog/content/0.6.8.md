## Devices (preview)

- Sync notes between your computers directly, without a cloud, from Settings → Devices (preview). Off until you turn it on; both devices need 0.6.8 or later
- Pair with a QR code or link, confirm the 6-digit code on both devices; removing a device is permanent
- Turning sync on changes how Notare stores notes, so it asks you to back up first

## Calendar

- Hide a whole provider (Apple, Google, Outlook or ICS files) from the calendar without disconnecting it; hidden providers are listed at the bottom so you can show them again

## App

- Settings → What's New shows the notes for every version instead of a blank page
