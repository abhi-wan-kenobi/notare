## Devices (preview)

- On macOS, 0.6.8 could not load the sync engine ("the database was opened without the cr-sqlite engine"), so sync could not be turned on. The app now lets the engine load
- When the engine still can't load, the Devices page shows the actual reason and keeps the switch off instead of failing when you flip it
