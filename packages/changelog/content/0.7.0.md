## A new look

- Warm, quiet, notes-first. Light theme by default; dark stays.
- The sidebar is now Home, Meetings, Calendar and Action items, your recent notes, and a Library for templates, snippets, contacts and trash. The bottom icon row is gone
- Press ⌘K / Ctrl+K for the command palette: actions, meetings, recipes
- Press ⌘J / Ctrl+J to open Ask Notare as a side panel instead of a floating window; the recording control and the ask box share one dock at the bottom

## Home

- What's today, what you recorded this week, open action items, and meetings that still need a summary, in one line
- Coming up: your next meetings with Open notes or Prepare

## Meetings

- Summary · Transcript · Action items · Recording tabs. Summary shows your notes, the AI summary at a glance, chapters you can click to jump the recording, and the decisions that were made
- Meetings and Action items pages list everything across notes, grouped by day and by meeting

## Recurring meetings

- Notare now recognises a meeting series. A series page shows action items carried over from earlier occurrences, what changed since last time, and every occurrence on a timeline
- Prepare the next occurrence with "From last time" already in your notes

## Calendar

- A month at a glance in the sidebar; a cleaner week view

## Settings

- Four hubs: General & System, AI & Speech, Integrations & Sync, Account & Storage
