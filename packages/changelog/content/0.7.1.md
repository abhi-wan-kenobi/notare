## Fixed

- 0.7.0 opened to a “Something went wrong” screen instead of the main window. The command palette was mounted outside the part of the app it depends on. If you are stuck on 0.7.0 and it does not update itself, download 0.7.1 from the releases page and install it over the top; your notes are untouched
- The command palette (⌘K / Ctrl+K) opened over the sidebar, half off-screen. It now opens centred over your notes
- Share and Schedule follow-up on a meeting page, and Today on the calendar, no longer sit underneath the Ask button
- The search box in the sidebar shows Ctrl+K on Windows and Linux instead of ⌘K

Everything new in 0.7.0 — the redesign, Home, the meeting page with chapters and decisions, recurring-meeting series, the command palette — is in this release.
