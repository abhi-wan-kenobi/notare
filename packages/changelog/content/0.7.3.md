## Meeting notes and updates

- Write and edit your own notes on Summary before, during, or after a meeting, without configuring AI or transcription. Leaving a note immediately after typing saves the final keystrokes
- Restart to update has its own bounded space in the main canvas, instead of being clipped behind the sidebar controls

## Hindi and Hinglish

- Mac builds include local Whisper with Metal acceleration, not just the Apple-specific speech runtimes
- Download full Whisper Large v3 (Multilingual, Q5) for a quality-first final pass. It is about 1 GB and slower than Turbo; use Small or Turbo for lighter live transcription
- Hinglish no longer forces multilingual Whisper to English only. Hindi and English are both considered per audio chunk; Hindi may be written in Devanagari

## Devices (preview)

- Long keys and pairing links stay inside the form and remain fully copyable
- QR pairing is easier to find. Expired offers, renewal, and confirmation errors are shown instead of retaining stale pairing state

## Floating controls

- One original compact orb instead of multiple shape and theme presets. Meeting recording and live dictation use the same design; colour stays in the waveform
- Meeting controls use a nonactivating panel on macOS so ordinary clicks do not bring the main window forward. Open app remains an explicit action

## Other repairs

- Mine shows actions assigned to you, not every contact in your address book
- Recurring meetings keep calendar-scoped identities and carried-over actions retain their original completion state instead of spawning independent copies
- Week view keeps standalone notes visible and no longer jumps back to the current hour every minute
- The dock does not offer to record into a meeting while its batch transcription is running
- Storage actions now say what they actually do: opening the notes folder or showing reset instructions does not claim to export everything or erase data
