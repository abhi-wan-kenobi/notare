## Meeting Summary

- Your manual notes are directly visible on Summary again, with no separate AI summary or Memos layer to open first
- Generated At a glance, chapters, and decisions stay inline alongside your notes
- A note that fails to save now tells you, and is retried instead of being dropped
- A meeting set to record automatically keeps trying on the next open if the recorder could not start

## Floating controls

- The floating recorder, dictation orb, and live captions use clear, readable surfaces in both light and dark theme
- Controls stay legible at small window sizes
- The recording bar no longer lingers after a recording stops, and captions collapse when live transcription is unavailable
- The dictation orb shows "Finishing dictation" while it wraps up

## Meetings, action items, and series

- The Meetings and Action items lists scroll when they are longer than the window
- Meeting rows show their date; month headings include the year for older meetings
- "Transcribed" replaces "Recorded" where the label reflects a transcript, not audio
- Overdue action items use your local date
- Series pages show summaries, topic changes, and decisions from past occurrences again
- "Prepare" reports a failure instead of doing nothing

## Transcript

- A failed import or re-transcription shows the error instead of a blank viewer
- Opening another meeting while one is recording no longer shows an empty transcript
- Space no longer hijacks buttons, the copy menu closes when the selection does, and speaker assignment cannot be submitted twice

## Safer actions

- Deleting a recording, deleting a local AI model, clearing dictation history, regenerating the MCP token, and hiding a whole recurring series now ask first
- Calendar menus say "Hide" for events that are only hidden in Notare

## Settings

- Saving an AI provider only reports success when the key was actually stored
- A failing sync, webhook, MCP, or Gmail section shows its own error instead of blanking the whole Integrations page
- Webhook and MCP settings report failures and reject invalid ports

## Command palette and onboarding

- "Search everything" stays available while you type, the theme toggle respects the system theme, and shortcuts that did not exist are no longer advertised
- Finishing onboarding cannot get stuck, and a failed storage-folder change is shown
