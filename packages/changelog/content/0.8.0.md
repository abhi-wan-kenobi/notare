## Speaker identity

- Assign a speaker to a person and choose **Remember voice** to learn from up to one minute of that speaker's recorded turns
- A contact's Voice section can import, replace, or forget a recording of that person
- On-device batch transcription names enrolled speakers when it recognizes them and adds them to the meeting; you can still correct any assignment

## Privacy and limits

- Voice profiles are stored only on this device and are excluded from device sync
- Forgetting a voice or deleting its contact permanently deletes the stored profile
- Voice matching is an aid, not proof of identity; recording quality, noise, and overlapping speech can affect the result
